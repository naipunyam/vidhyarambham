## Learning science (summaries and studies)
- v https://www.learningscientists.org/downloadable-materials Learning Scientists: posters, slides, bookmarks, sticker templates for the six strategies (spaced practice, retrieval practice, elaboration, interleaving, concrete examples, dual coding); CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; "use our materials ... for educational purposes", attribute, don't modify, non-commercial.
- v https://www.learningscientists.org/translations posters in 12 languages, slides in 5; NOT Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil or Kannada.
- v https://www.learningscientists.org/retrieval-practice page with posters, slides, bookmarks, video, blog posts.
- v https://www.learningscientists.org/blog/2019/2/21-1 (21 Feb 2019, Kuepper-Tetzel): Mueller & Oppenheimer 2014 longhand > laptop not replicated by Morehead, Dunlosky & Rawson 2019; content of notes matters more than method; no blanket rule.
- v https://www.aft.org/ae/fall2013/dunlosky Dunlosky, American Educator Fall 2013 "Strengthening the Student Toolbox": practice testing and distributed practice "most effective"; interleaved practice, elaborative interrogation, self-explanation "promising"; rereading, highlighting, summarization, keyword mnemonic, imagery for text "less useful".
- v https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/journals/pspi/learning-techniques.html APS page for Dunlosky et al. 2013 PSPI (links to full text PDF/HTML): practice tests and distributed practice high utility; underlining, rereading, mnemonics surprisingly low utility.
- v https://ncase.me/remember/ Nicky Case, "How To Remember Anything Forever-ish" (Oct 2018): interactive comic on spaced repetition, Leitner box, forgetting curve; 20 minutes; PUBLIC DOMAIN.
- v https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0120644 Murre & Dros 2015 PLOS ONE (CC BY): Ebbinghaus forgetting curve replicated; jump upward at 24 h point (possibly sleep; not proven).
- v https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/psy_facpub/1765/ Pashler, McDaniel, Rohrer, Bjork 2008 PSPI 9(3):105-119 "Learning Styles: Concepts and Evidence": "no adequate evidence base to justify incorporating learning-styles assessments into general educational practice".
- v https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/speed-reading-promises-are-too-good-to-be-true-scientists-find.html (14 Jan 2016) APS on Rayner et al. 2016 PSPI: trade-off between speed and accuracy; eye movements not the bottleneck; skimming loses depth; best route: become a better reader (practice, vocabulary).
- v https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2009/08/090825113133.htm (26 Aug 2009) Ophir, Nass, Wagner PNAS: heavy media multitaskers worse at filtering irrelevant information, at keeping items sorted in memory, and at task switching.
- v https://psychology.ecu.edu/wp-content/pv-uploads/sites/216/2019/03/Roediger-Karpicke-2006.pdf Roediger & Karpicke 2006 Psych Science: studying better at 5 min; testing much better at 2 days and 1 week; Exp 2: STTT 61% vs SSSS 40% after a week; repeated studying raised confidence though it performed worst.
- v https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/research/2011/110120KarpickeScience.html (20 Jan 2011) Karpicke & Blunt Science: retrieval practice beat concept mapping (about 50% better long-term retention, press release); students predicted concept mapping would be best.
- v https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006: 94 independent tests; d = .65 medium-to-large effect on goal attainment; free PDF (Open Access Green).
- v https://www.surrey.ac.uk/news/does-it-really-take-66-days-form-habit-we-asked-expert-dr-pippa-lally (21 Jan 2026): average 66 days to form a daily habit; range 18 to 254 days.
- v https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/becoming-an-expert-takes-more-than-practice.html (2 Jul 2014) Macnamara, Hambrick, Oswald: deliberate practice explains about 12% of performance differences overall: games 26%, music 21%, sports 18%, education 4%, professions <1%.
- v https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5439266/ Dresler et al. 2017 Neuron: 40 x 30 min method-of-loci training over six weeks improved memory in naive participants; persisted 4 months.
- v https://www.unh.edu/teaching-learning-resource-hub/sites/default/files/media/2023-06/itow-learning-by-teaching-fiorella.pdf Fiorella, "Learning by Teaching" (APA Div 2 e-book, 2023): preparing to teach helps somewhat (mixed); explaining to others adds more; Fiorella & Mayer: actually teaching best on delayed test; most effective as a retrieval activity (explain without notes); low stakes; support needed.
- v https://www.retrievalpractice.org/ Pooja Agarwal: 100+ free resources, research database.
- vr https://www.coursera.org/learn/learning-how-to-learn Oakley & Sejnowski: "Enroll for free" (certificate paid); 4 modules (~15 h); focused/diffuse modes, chunking, illusions of competence, procrastination and memory (memory palace); 25 languages available.
