## India law and ethics
- v https://www.ascionline.in/social/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ASCI-Influencer-Guidelines.pdf ASCI Guidelines for Influencer Advertising in Digital Media (Addendum 1 15.07.2021; Addendum 2 17.08.2023): material connection = payment, free products (even unsolicited), discounts, gifts, contest entries, trips, media barters, awards, family/employment ties. "Disclosures are required even if the evaluations are unbiased." Labels: Advertisement, Ad, Sponsored, Collaboration, Partnership, Employee, Free gift, Affiliate (+ platform tags Paid Partnership / Includes Paid Promotion). Upfront & prominent, "should not be buried in a group of hashtags or links"; likely missed in bios, end of posts, behind MORE. Live: announce at start and end (+caption). Stories/images without text: superimpose label. Audio: start and end and around breaks. Video <=15 s: label >=3 s; 15 s-2 min: one-third of length; >=2 min: whole section mentioning brand. Language: English or the language of the ad. Influencer responsible for disclosure; check advertiser can substantiate claims. Virtual influencers must disclose not human. Health/finance (Addendum 2): need qualifications (SEBI registration for stock/investment; IRDAI/CA/CS; medical/nursing/nutrition etc.), shown prominently.
