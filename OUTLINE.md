# Kerala Real Estate Business: From Token Advance to Registration — outline

Course id `kerala-real-estate`; section ids `re-...`; lesson codes RE-Mxx-Lyy (stable).

## Assumptions
- No real estate course existed in the app.
- Low-capital service paths first; investing later.
- Rules dated Nov 2026, marked check current rules; official Kerala portals block bots, so details cross-checked with secondary sources and marked.
- Income-tax Act 2025 in force from 1 April 2026; section numbers changed.

## Front/back pages
Start, schedule, Week 0 setup, baseline quiz (form A), weekly market track with milestones, toolkit, part pages A–C, final retest (form B) and checklist, what next, glossary with Malayalam, sources.
