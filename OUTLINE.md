# Practical Learning Capabilities course (LRN): outline

Course id `learning-capabilities`; section ids `lrn-...`; lesson codes LRN-Mxx-Lyy. Saved before any module was written.

## Shape

- Week 0 (setup and baseline tests), then 28 part-time weeks at about 5-7 hours (target 6.0 h, total about 160 h). Full-time: weekLength 0.37, about 10-11 calendar weeks at about 15 h; the 30-day sprint starts after Module 12 and runs alongside Modules 13-20.
- Modules are ordered so the PSC learning system starts early: strategies and AnkiDroid in month 1, focus, habits and metacognition in month 2, exam mastery by week 15, so the 12-week PSC plan runs during the rest of the course.
- Tracks: weekly learning review (20 min, from week 1); the 30-day AnkiDroid deck review (starts week 4, day 30 about week 8).
- Baseline tests in week 0, repeated in the final week with parallel forms: 20-item list recall after 10 minutes and 24 hours; reading speed with a comprehension quiz (effective reading rate); focus duration; digit span forward and backward.
- Learning Workbook (Google Sheets) tabs grow module by module: Baseline, Study Log, Revision Schedule, Error Log, PYQ Analysis, Syllabus Map, Weekly Review, Focus Log, Sprint.
- Required practical work: memory palace (M11-L02), 100+ card AnkiDroid deck reviewed 30 days (M04 + track), five recorded Feynman explanations (M08 project), PYQ analysis and error log (M12-L02, L03), complete 12-week PSC plan (M12-L05), teaching a family member or study partner (M20-L01), 30-day capstone sprint with before/after tests and reflection (M21).
