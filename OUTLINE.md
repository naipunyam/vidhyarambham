# Personal finance course: outline (saved before any module was written)

Course id `personal-finance`, file `public/courses/personal-finance.json`, format `vidhyarambham-course` v1.
Title: Practical Personal Finance for India: Budget, Protect, Invest and Plan.
Lesson codes FIN-Mxx-Lyy (ids fin-mxx-lyy); module ids fin-mxx; other ids start with `fin-`.
Facts: FACTS.md (all checked 2026-11-03). Course stamp: "as of November 2026", "check current rules".

## Paces
- Part-time: Week 0 + 31 weeks, about 5 hours a week (weekLength 1).
- Full-time: about 10 calendar weeks, about 14 hours a week (weekLength 0.33). The 30-day expense log keeps its 30 real days.

## Front matter
1. fin-start: Start here (what you build, honest expectations, neutrality, assumptions, module map, cross-references)
2. fin-schedule: weekly schedule, both paces, PSC rhythm, exam mode
3. fin-w0: Week 0: Money Folder, Money Workbook (Expenses tab), phone and account security, rules from day 1, AI coach prompt, calendar
4. fin-baseline: 20 skills rated 1-5, money snapshot, three sentences (repeated in week 31)
5. fin-habits: Money habits track: 30-day expense log (weeks 1-5: day 7, 14, 21, 30) and monthly money reviews (weeks 9, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29)

## Rules
Educational only: no named funds, stocks, policies or insurers as recommendations. Teach evaluation with official data.
Every rate, limit and rule: dated, linked, "check current rules". No invented links or figures.
Examples: made-up people and numbers (Anu, PSC aspirant in Kakkattil; Rahul, new Kerala government LD clerk; Fathima, freelance designer in Kuttiady; Nisha's Oven home bakery).
