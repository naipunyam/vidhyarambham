# Practical Social Media Marketing: Grow an Account, Run Campaigns, Get Clients — outline

Course id `social-media-marketing`, version 1, file `public/courses/social-media-marketing.json`.
Paces: Part-time (weekLength 1, ~6 h/week, 40 weeks + Week 0, about 9.5 months) and
Full-time (weekLength 0.35, ~17 h/week, about 14 calendar weeks).
Week 0 = setup. totalWeeks = 40. Facts checked 2026-10-25; platform facts stamped "as of October 2026".

Why 40 weeks: growth needs real days. The 30-day challenge (weeks 14–18), the 4-week pilot for a
real business (weeks 29–32) and the 90-day growth sprint (weeks 27–39) all run in real days.
Full-time: study moves ~2.9 course weeks per calendar week, but those real-day projects keep their
length and overlap later modules; the full-time schedule table says when to start each one.
The full-time growth project = the whole posting period from the end of Module 3 (about 78 days).

Default practice account (assumption, changeable): a hyperlocal Malayalam–English page about your
area (Kuttiady and nearby): shops, food, events, people, useful updates. Reasons: endless nearby
content, faster local growth, every post builds a relationship with a future client, and it becomes
your portfolio. Alternatives: a hobby/interest niche, Kerala PSC study tips, or your own brand.
