## Key assumptions

- No Personal Development or Practical Psychology course exists in the app, so this is a new course (checked index.json: ai-generalist, entrepreneurship, social-media-marketing, personal-finance, learning-capabilities).
- Rejection therapy gets its own module (M14) right after Confidence and courage, so the course has 36 modules: the 35 topics in the brief plus the dedicated rejection-therapy module; the capstone is M36.
- Study load is about 5.5 hours a week part-time; daily practices (habit tracker, meditation, workouts, challenges) are 10 to 40 minutes a day on top and are the point of the course.
- Topics already taught in Learning Capabilities (procrastination, focus, sleep for learning, exam anxiety, biases for PSC) are cross-referenced, not repeated.
- Each part starts in a fresh week and has a part page with a standalone start (mini-baseline and setup) so it can be taken alone.
- Evidence ratings: Strong / Moderate / Mixed or weak / Failed to replicate or myth / Practical tool.
